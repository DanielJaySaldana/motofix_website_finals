How MotoFix
works

  1. 01

    Create your rider account

    Choose Rider and provide your name and contact information.

  2. 02

    Add your motorcycle

    Save your motorcycle details so they’re ready for your next booking.

  3. 03

    Find a motor shop

    Explore the map, compare services, and choose a nearby shop.

  4. 04

    Book a service

    Select your motorcycle and service, describe the issue, choose a date and time, then review your booking.

  5. 05

    Track the repair

    Check your booking status and repair progress, and contact the shop when needed.

  6. 06

    Review the work and costs

    See the completed repair details, parts, labor, and total cost in your service history.

  7. 07

    Buy parts when needed

    Browse available parts and review your order before checkout.

  8. 08

    Monitor your expenses

    Use the expense tracker to review motorcycle spending and plan future maintenance.

  1. 01

    Create your motor shop account

    Choose Motor Shop and provide the owner’s contact information.

  2. 02

    Set up your shop profile

    Add your shop details, location, services, and business documents.

  3. 03

    Organize your team and branches

    Create mechanic accounts and set up branch information if you have multiple locations.

  4. 04

    Add parts and inventory

    List motorcycle parts, set prices and stock levels, and manage sales.

  5. 05

    Manage incoming bookings

    Review service requests, organize the schedule, and assign a mechanic.

  6. 06

    Oversee repair progress

    Track assigned jobs and keep repair statuses and work details up to date.

  7. 07

    Complete the service record

    Document the work, parts, labor, and costs so the rider can review the completed repair.

  8. 08

    Review and export records

    Use analytics and branch views to review performance, then export business records through App Integrations.

  1. 01

    Receive your staff account

    Your shop owner creates the mechanic account and provides your login details.

  2. 02

    Review your dashboard

    See pending tasks, active repairs, and the work scheduled for you.

  3. 03

    Open an assigned task

    Check the motorcycle, issue, instructions, priority, and deadline.

  4. 04

    Inspect the repair details

    Review the job information before starting the work.

  5. 05

    Update the repair

    Record its status, progress, services, and parts used.

  6. 06

    Add work notes and costs

    Document the diagnosis, labor, parts costs, and other repair notes.

  7. 07

    Coordinate with the team

    Use the repair contacts to reach the shop owner or rider when clarification is needed.

  8. 08

    Review your work history

    Return to earlier assignments and completed repairs for reference.
